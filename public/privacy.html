Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

This explains what information GongGo collects, why, who sees it, and what you can do about it. We collect as little as we can, we don't sell anything about you, and there are no ads or tracking.

1. Who we are

GongGo is run by Dipanker Baral in New South Wales, Australia. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988. Contact us about privacy at [contact email].

2. What we collect

Your account
Your email address, and your account ID from our sign-in service. If you sign in with Google or Apple, they share your email and, if you allow it, your name.
Your name
The first name you choose. Other players see it.
Your age confirmation
We ask for your date of birth once, only to check you're 18 or over. We don't store it; we record only that you confirmed, and when.
What you do on GongGo
Games you post (title, sport, time, place, map pin and notes), games you join, comments, and reports you make.
Moderation records
Reports about you or your content, and any bans.
Technical information
Like most websites, our servers log requests, including IP addresses and browser type, to keep GongGo working and secure. These logs are deleted after about 7 days.

We don't collect your location. Map pins show where games are, not where you are.

3. Why we use it

  • to run your account and let you post, join and comment on games
  • to show other players who's coming to a game
  • to keep GongGo safe: moderation, stopping spam, and protecting accounts
  • to tell you about your games (for example, if one is cancelled), once we add notifications

We don't sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

4. Who can see what

  • Everyone can see games, including the host's and players' first names.
  • Players in a game can see its comments.
  • GongGo admins can see reports and take moderation action.
  • Nobody else sees your email address.

5. Services we use

  • Amazon Web Services hosts GongGo and runs sign-in. Your information is stored in AWS's Sydney region, in Australia.
  • Google and Apple, only if you choose to sign in with them. They may process information overseas under their own privacy policies.
  • OpenStreetMap provides the map images. When a map loads, your browser asks OpenStreetMap's servers (which may be overseas) for those images, which shares your IP address with them.

6. Cookies and storage

GongGo stores your sign-in in your browser so you stay signed in. Our sign-in pages use cookies for the same reason. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, or analytics.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete your account, name, the games you host, your comments and reports, and your place in other games, straight away. Copies in our backups disappear within 7 days. If an account was banned, we keep a record of the ban (an account ID only) so the ban stays in place.

8. Your choices and rights

  • See or correct your information: change your name from your account menu, or ask us for a copy of what we hold.
  • Delete everything: use Delete my account in My games.
  • Complain: contact us first, and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

9. Security

Information is encrypted in transit and stored encrypted. Passwords are handled by our sign-in service and never seen by GongGo. If a breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we'll tell you and the Information Commissioner as the law requires.

10. Children

GongGo is for people 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information about anyone younger, and we'll delete any account we find belongs to a child.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll ask you to accept the new version. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
