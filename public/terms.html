Last updated 2 October 2026

GongGo helps people around the Illawarra find and join pickup games and community sport. These terms are the rules for using it. They're written to be read, so please read them.

1. Who we are

GongGo is a community project run by Dipanker Baral ("we" or "us") in New South Wales, Australia. You can contact us at [contact email].

2. You must be 18 or over

GongGo is for adults. By creating an account you confirm you are 18 or older. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up to check this; we don't keep it. If we find out an account belongs to someone under 18, we'll close it.

3. Your account

  • Keep your sign-in details to yourself. You're responsible for what happens on your account.
  • Use a name other players will recognise. Don't pretend to be someone else.
  • You can delete your account at any time from My games.

4. GongGo is a noticeboard, not an organiser

Games on GongGo are organised by the people who post them ("hosts"), not by us. We don't run, supervise, check or insure any game, venue or person. Any arrangement to play is between the people involved.

5. Playing sport has risks

Sport and physical activity can lead to injury. You take part in any game at your own risk. Only join games that suit your fitness and experience, look after yourself and others, and follow the rules of the venue. If in doubt about your health, check with a doctor first.

6. If you host a game

  • Describe it accurately: the sport, time, place and anything players should know.
  • Make sure you're allowed to use the venue. Some parks and courts need a booking with the council or the venue, especially for organised or paid activities.
  • Update or cancel your game promptly if plans change.
  • Treat everyone fairly. You can remove a player from your game, but don't do it to discriminate against anyone.

7. Community rules

When you post a game, comment or otherwise use GongGo, don't:

  • harass, threaten, abuse or discriminate against anyone
  • post anything illegal, false or misleading, or anything sexual or violent
  • share someone else's personal information without their permission
  • advertise or sell things (spam), or post games that aren't real
  • try to break, overload or get around GongGo's security, limits or moderation

Anyone can report a game or comment. We may remove content, remove games, or suspend or ban accounts that break these rules, and we decide what breaks them.

8. What you post

You own what you post. By posting it, you let us show it on GongGo to the people it's meant for (for example, a game to everyone, and comments to the players in that game), and keep the copies we need to run and protect the service. You're responsible for what you post.

9. The service

We work to keep GongGo running and accurate, but we provide it as it is. Games may be cancelled, information may be out of date, and the service may sometimes be unavailable. We may change, pause or stop parts of GongGo, and will try to give notice of big changes. GongGo is currently free. If we add paid features, such as tournament fees, we'll update these terms first.
